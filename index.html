About

Based on the Kalamazoo Bicycle Club's K'zoo Region Bikeways Map (Dec 2024), made with RCKC and KATS. The October 2026 update adds facilities opened since then and projects under construction or planned through 2030, each with its status and sources. Planned dates can slip. Ride at your own risk.
