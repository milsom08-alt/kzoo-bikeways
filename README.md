# K'zoo Region Bikeways Map – October 2026 update

An interactive web map of bikeways in the Kalamazoo, Michigan region: shared-use paths, bike lanes, bike routes, paved shoulders, recommended roads and gravel routes.

It is based on the Kalamazoo Bicycle Club's *K'zoo Region Bikeways Map* (last updated December 2024, made with RCKC and KATS). The October 2026 update adds:

- **New and open since Dec 2024:** Portage St bike lanes (Cork St to Stockbridge Ave), Fruit Belt Rail Trail (Oshtemo), East Central Trail (Portage), KL Ave path under US-131.
- **Existing but missing from the Dec 2024 map:** Vicksburg Trail north end to the Portage city limit, E Kilgore Rd bike lanes (Sprinkle–Portage Rd), Gull Rd path east segment.
- **Under construction / 2026:** Austin Lake Trail (Portage, full route from the city's plan), KRVT Galesburg to Augusta, KL Ave trail (Copper Beech to US-131), Kalamazoo Ave reconstruction zone.
- **Planned 2027–2030:** city of Kalamazoo and Portage bikeway projects, and Road Commission of Kalamazoo County shoulder-widening projects.

Every added item has its status, dates and sources in its popup. Planned dates can change. Use at your own risk.

## Files

- `index.html` – the map (Leaflet, OpenStreetMap/CARTO basemap)
- `data/bikeways.json` – map data used by the page
- `data/Kzoo-Region-Bikeways-Map-Oct2026.kml` – full map for Google Earth
- `data/Kzoo-Map-Oct2026-Layers.zip` – one KML per layer, for importing into Google My Maps
